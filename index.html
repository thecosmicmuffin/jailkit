00 Why this never leaves your device
Read this once. Then you don't have to trust us.
There is no server
This page is one HTML file. No account, no login, no database, nothing on our end to log. Once the page has loaded it can work with the network switched off — try it in airplane mode.
What you type stays in the page
Every answer lives in this tab's memory. It is never transmitted as you type — no autosave to anywhere, no analytics on this page.
Saving writes to this browser only
Save here puts the sheet in this browser's own local storage, on this device. Another browser, another phone, or a private window cannot see it. Wipe deletes it.
Nothing is sent until you send it
The only way data moves is a button you press: copy, download, text, email, or the one-time link. You choose the channel and the recipient every time.
The one-time link is a paste, not an upload
We copy your text to your clipboard and open onetimesecret.com. Nothing is transmitted until you paste it there yourself and click Create. We never post on your behalf.
Check for yourself
Open your browser's developer tools, Network tab, and reload. You will see the page and its font load. After that, typing produces no requests at all.
The one risk this cannot fix
A saved sheet is readable by anyone holding this unlocked device — including at a search. If that is a concern, do not tap Save, or tap Wipe before you leave.
